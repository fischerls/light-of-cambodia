# How to Update the Light of Cambodia Website

You can update the website yourself. You do it by asking Claude, the same AI helper you already
use. You type what you want changed, in plain words. Claude makes the change. You look at a
preview. When it looks right, Larry puts it live.

**You cannot break the live website.** Every change goes to a preview page first. The real site
only changes after Larry approves it. So relax and try things.

---

## One-time setup (we do this together on our video call)

You only do this part once.

1. Make a free GitHub account at **github.com**. GitHub is the safe box where the website files live.
2. Accept the invite Larry sends you. It arrives by email from GitHub.
3. Go to **claude.ai/code** and sign in with your Claude account.
4. Connect Claude to GitHub, and pick the project called **light-of-cambodia**.

Larry will walk you through every click on the call.

---

## How to make a change (any time)

1. Go to **claude.ai/code** and sign in.
2. Open the **light-of-cambodia** project.
3. Type what you want changed, like you are texting a helper. For example:
   - "On the Our Team page, change Sok Chea's title to Program Director."
   - "Add this new photo to the Our Impact page." (You can attach photos.)
   - "Fix the spelling of 'Battambang' on the How We Work page."
4. Claude does the work and gives you a **preview link**. Click it. That is a private copy of the
   site with your change on it.
5. Look at the preview. Is it right?
   - **Yes?** Tell Claude *"looks good, open a pull request and send it to Larry."* Larry reviews it,
     approves it, and it goes live. Usually the same day.
   - **No?** Tell Claude what to fix. "Make the photo bigger." "That's the wrong name." It tries
     again and gives you a new preview link.

---

## Tips for asking

- Say **which page**. That is the single most helpful thing.
- **One change at a time** works best.
- Be **exact** with names, dates, and numbers. Claude uses exactly what you type.
- For photos, **attach the photo** and say where it goes.
- Not sure how to word it? Describe it like you would to a person. That works.

---

## What Claude will not do (on purpose)

These are locked so nothing important gets damaged. If you truly need one changed, ask Larry.

- **The logo never changes.** Not the shape, not the colors.
- **The site colors and fonts never change.** They come from the brand book.
- **The donate form is not touched.** Donations keep working.
- **The prayer form's behind-the-scenes part is not touched.**
- **Nothing goes straight to the live site.** Preview first, Larry second.
