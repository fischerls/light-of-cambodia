01 — The Brand

At a Glance

Light of Cambodia is a Christ-centered nonprofit walking alongside children, families, and local leaders in rural Cambodia, sharing the hope of the gospel through long-term, relational ministry. The brand should feel warm, hopeful, rooted, and human. Sunlight over rice fields, not corporate charity.

Brand feel

  • Warm and personal. Real photography of real people and places in Pursat.
  • Rooted. Deep greens ground the brand; flame and marigold bring light and hope.
  • Quietly confident. Generous whitespace, serif headlines, no visual noise.

Symbolism in the mark

  • Cross (marigold gold): Christ at the center.
  • Flame (vermilion): the light, the Spirit, hope.
  • Laurel of leaves (green): growth, harvest, the rice fields of Pursat.
  • Butterflies: new life, transformation.

Bilingual identity

The brand lives in English and Khmer. The Khmer name ពន្លឺនៃកម្ពុជា appears alongside the English wordmark in hero and formal contexts. Always set Khmer text in Noto Serif Khmer, never in a Latin font's fallback.

02 — Logo

The Mark & How to Use It

The master asset is a full-color mark on a transparent background (logo.png, 500×500). On photography or dark backgrounds, always place it inside a solid white circle. The artwork sits at ~118% of the circle diameter so the laurel reads clearly (the source file carries generous transparent padding — never letterbox it).

Do

  • Keep clear space around the mark of at least ¼ of its diameter.
  • Minimum size: 48px / 0.5in diameter on screen and print.
  • Pair with the wordmark set in Young Serif ("Light of / Cambodia" on two lines is the house style).
  • Use the white-circle treatment over photos and evergreen backgrounds.

Don't

  • Recolor, outline, or add effects to the mark. The logo is sacred — use the supplied file only.
  • Stretch, rotate, or crop into the artwork.
  • Place the full-color mark directly on busy photography without the white circle.
  • Redraw the cross, flame, laurel, or butterflies.

Rules

  • Young Serif has one weight (400). Never fake-bold or italicize it.
  • Headlines set tight (line-height ~1.12) and are never all-caps.
  • Fallbacks: Georgia/serif for Young Serif; system-ui/sans-serif for Alegreya Sans.

05 — Graphic Elements

Buttons, Shapes & Photography

Buttons — always pill-shaped (999px radius)

Give Now Get Involved See Our Work

Flame (#C74E33, white text) is the primary action, reserved for giving/donation CTAs. Gold (#E8AF4F, Evergreen Deep text) is secondary; hovers to Sunlight #FEE5B3. Ghost (2px current-color border) sits on photos and dark sections. Labels are Alegreya Sans Bold. Focus rings are 3px Marigold.

Shape language

  • Cards and images: 14px corner radius.
  • Buttons and tags: full pill (999px).
  • The circle is a brand motif (logo circle, radial glows). No sharp-cornered boxes.
  • Motion eases with cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) — soft, settling movement.

Photography

  • Documentary, warm, golden-hour light. Children, families, rice fields, real ministry moments in Pursat.
  • No stock-photo gloss, no posed corporate imagery.
  • Crop ratios in use: 4:4.6 portraits, 4:3 project cards.
  • Under text: evergreen scrim gradient (see page 5). Standalone: no filter beyond gentle warmth.

Dark sections

Full-bleed Evergreen #12361E panels are a signature move (hero, verse band, footer). Text is On Dark #F7F2E3 / On Dark Soft #DAD4BF, accents are Marigold, and a soft marigold radial glow may rise from an edge. Oversized watermark Khmer script at very low opacity is an approved texture.
