# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

US-based Christian donors, church mission boards, and praying partners. They land here from word-of-mouth, a church bulletin, or a leader's Facebook share. Their job: understand what Light of Cambodia is, trust it, then give, pray, or reach out. Secondary: Cambodian partners checking legitimacy.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Light of Cambodia, a Christ-centered nonprofit serving rural communities in Pursat, Cambodia (children & youth ministry, family & community care, church & leadership partnership). Success = a visitor feels the warmth and integrity of the ministry within one scroll and clicks Give Now (Zeffy) or emails lightofcambodia.loc@gmail.com.

## Brand Personality

Sunlit, steadfast, humble. The emotional register is dawn light over rice fields: hopeful and warm, never slick or corporate-NGO. Gospel-forward without being preachy; dignity-first imagery (their own real photos, never poverty-porn stock).

## Anti-references

- Generic NGO template: full-width stock photo + orange DONATE + three icon cards + progress bars.
- Charity-water-clone minimalism; guilt-driven dark imagery.
- Cream/beige AI-default landing page with terracotta accent.
- Corporate church-plant sites with gradient purple.

## Design Principles

1. Light is the thesis: golden light in imagery, glow, and palette carries the name.
2. Their real photos over any stock; the people ARE the brand.
3. Bilingual identity: Khmer script (Noto Serif Khmer) as the one deliberate kicker system, honoring the place.
4. Logo is sacred: use as-is (transparent PNG derived, artwork untouched); its green/flame/gold ARE the palette.
5. One flame-colored action: Give Now. Everything else stays green/gold.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: 4.5:1 body text, visible focus, reduced-motion alternatives, semantic landmarks, alt text written with dignity.
