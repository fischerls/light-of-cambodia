ដំណើរបេសកកម្មMission Trips

Come with us in January

Each January, a team from Light of Cambodia travels to Pursat to serve alongside the local pastors, teachers, and leaders who are already doing this work, week after week.

ដើរជាមួយគ្នា

Joining work already underway

Christ is already at work in the heart of the Cambodian people. The ministry in Pursat runs all year, led by Seng Sophanna and the Cambodia team.

Going is a chance to show up in person for relationships that carry on the other fifty-one weeks of the year, and to bring real, tangible support to the children and families we walk alongside.

Interested in coming?

You do not need a ministry title or a special skill set. Travelers help with children's lessons, spend time with families, and support the local team in whatever the week calls for.

Everyone who travels works through a traveler packet first: a release form, emergency contacts, a field commitment policy, safeguarding training, and our guides on speaking with dignity and using photos well. We would rather prepare people properly than send them unprepared.
